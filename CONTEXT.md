# Realms governance language

Terminology for the treasury and grants governance design. These definitions describe the design vocabulary; they do not imply that the proposed system is deployed.

## Language

**Voting entitlement**: The influence a participant receives from the eligibility and weighting rules for a particular ballot, before delegation.

**Participant**: The governance subject whose eligible holdings and activity are combined before weighting. Whether this subject is an address or a recognized person remains an open design decision.

**Delegation mandate**: A participant's time-limited authorization for a delegate to exercise their voting entitlement within a specified governance scope.

**Delegate**: A representative authorized to cast ballots for other participants. Representation does not transfer ownership of the underlying assets.

**Governance snapshot**: The fixed eligibility, ownership, delegation, and scoring state used for a ballot.

**Square-root weighting**: A voting-power rule that gives diminishing additional influence as a participant's eligible holdings increase.

**Quadratic voting**: A ballot mechanism in which expressing a number of votes on an option costs the square of that number in voice credits.

**Governance XP**: A proposed record of qualifying participation used as an input to governance influence. Its source and earning rules are not yet defined.

**Grant round**: A bounded funding decision with a published budget, eligible applications, selection rules, and payment conditions.
